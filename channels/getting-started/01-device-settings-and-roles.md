# Device Settings & Roles
Within the greater Phoenix & Tucson metro areas, the Arizona Meshtastic Community does **not** use the standard Meshtastic defaults of Long-Fast/20 (default presets on new devices and firmware). If you are **outside** the metro areas of Phoenix & Tucson, there is a probability these rural areas have not made the switch to our modified settings, and it may be in your best interest to continue to use the Meshtastic default settings.
## Recommended Settings for Phoenix & Tucson Metro Areas
- **Preset:** {{METRO_PRESET}}  
- **Frequency Slot:** {{METRO_FREQ_SLOT}}  
- **Okay to MQTT:** Checked On (allows your node to send basic diagnostic info that helps the community understand network behavior and troubleshoot when needed.)
## Recommended Settings for Outside of Phoenix & Tucson
- **Preset:** Long-Fast  
- **Frequency Slot:** 20  
- **Okay to MQTT:** Checked On (allows your node to send basic diagnostic info that helps the community understand network behavior and troubleshoot when needed.)

Using these settings ensures your node works smoothly with the rest of the Arizona networks. If you are in need of assistance setting up your nodes with your device, please visit our https://discord.com/channels/1270431345734062133/1390259006949560393 section for step-by-step guides for your device, or reach out to a member of the community.
