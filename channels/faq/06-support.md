# Support

## Who should I contact if I need help?
If you’re not sure who to reach out to, this is a good rule to follow:

- **Getting started, general questions, moderation, or day-to-day help**  
  → Reach out to a **Community Ambassador (CA)**

- **Local advice, node setup help, or best practices**  
  → A **Community Ambassador (CA)** is usually the right place to start

- **Mountaintop deployments, permanent infrastructure, or major network changes**  
  → Contact the **Infrastructure Leadership Team (ILT)**

- **Serious server issues or platform-level problems**  
  → Contact the **Community Administrative Team (CAT)**

If you’re ever unsure, just ask in a public channel. Someone will help point you in the right direction.
