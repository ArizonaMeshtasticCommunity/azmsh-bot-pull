# Community Roles & Leadership  

The Arizona Meshtastic Community is run by volunteers who care about both the network and the people using it. As the community has grown, we’ve put a few leadership roles in place to help keep things organized, fair, and running smoothly.

These roles aren’t about control. They exist to support the community, protect the network, and make sure everyone has a good experience.

## Community Administrative Team (CAT)
The **Community Administrative Team (<@&1270431547505381496>)** helps guide the overall direction of AZMSH and keeps the community’s core systems running.

CAT focuses on:
- Community-wide policies and long-term direction
- Managing and maintaining shared platforms like Discord
- Handling serious issues that require elevated access or final decisions
- Supporting the other leadership teams when escalation is needed

CAT acts as the final decision-maker when issues can’t be resolved elsewhere, they monitor conversations and help whenever possible.

## Community Ambassador (CA)
**<@&1270460470708666429> (CAs)** are the people you’ll interact with most often.

They are here to:
- Help new members get started
- Answer questions and provide guidance
- Moderate conversations and keep things respectful
- De-escalate issues before they become bigger problems
- Point people in the right direction when help is needed

CAs are community-focused and approachable. If you’re unsure who to talk to, a CA is usually the best first stop.
