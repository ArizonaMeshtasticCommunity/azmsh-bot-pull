## Infrastructure Leadership Team (ILT)
The **Infrastructure Leadership Team (<@&1361378794853634179>)** focuses on the technical side of the network.

ILT members help with:
- Reviewing and coordinating mountaintop and high-impact deployments
- Maintaining technical standards for infrastructure nodes
- Monitoring overall network health and performance
- Keeping documentation and infrastructure records up to date
- Advising on long-term technical planning

ILT does not handle moderation or community discipline. Their focus is keeping the mesh stable, reliable, and growing in a healthy way.
