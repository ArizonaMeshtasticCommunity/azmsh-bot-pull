**Welcome to the Arizona Meshtastic Community!**

Because we are a community revolving around Meshtastic, I have `borrowed` their Rules posting from their server. We stand by their Code of Conduct, and their rules apply here as well.

When you first join, you might not see all our channels. Check out the <id:browse> pane or right-click on the server name and select "Show All Channels" to explore everything this community has to offer. Happy meshing!

**Community Rules**

1. **Be Respectful and Inclusive**: Treat everyone with kindness and respect. Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech of any kind will not be tolerated.
2. **Keep It Appropriate**: Avoid sharing any inappropriate, sexually suggestive, or disturbing content.
3. **Foster a Positive Environment**: Engage in constructive discussions; avoid personal attacks, insults, and negativity.
4. **No Politics**: Politics is a divisive topic that too often creates hostility and fractures communities. To keep AZMSH inclusive, friendly, and collaborative, political posts of any kind are not permitted. It's OK to talk about the policies, but let's be careful to not let the conversation devolve into politics.
5. **Respect Privacy**: Do not share personal information about others without their consent.
6. **No Spam or Disruption**: Refrain from spamming, excessive self-promotion, or other disruptive behavior.
7. **No Precompiled Binary or Zip File Sharing:** Only those with the Admin, Leads, or Developer roles may upload precompiled binaries or zip files directly to Discord. This excludes sharing links to Meshtastic’s official GitHub, where approved binaries are available for download. This rule is in place to maintain security and reduce potential risks.
8. **This server exists to support Meshtastic users and development:** General discussion, troubleshooting, and experimentation should remain Meshtastic-focused. Extended discussion, promotion, or advocacy of other LoRa mesh platforms belongs in their respective communities.
9. **Report Issues**: If you see any rule violations or experience harassment, report it privately to <@882066290532048916>, <@429045828435443712>, or <@90687618404061184>.

Focus on Our Strengths - This community thrives when we center our discussions on what unites us: radios, technology, 3D printing, cars, tools, food, and countless other shared interests. These are the things that connect us, spark innovation, and create lasting friendships.

Self Promotion: The <#1508844505477021796> section is a dedicated place to share their projects, channels, websites, shops, events, content, and other work without overwhelming the rest of the server. There is a requirement to be present in the server for more then 30 days to prevent spam.

**Note**: These rules align with Meshtastic's [Code of Conduct](https://meshtastic.org/docs/legal/conduct/) and may be updated as needed. Admins reserve the right to enforce these rules, including corrections, warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans as outlined in Meshtastic's [Enforcement Guidelines](https://meshtastic.org/docs/legal/conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
