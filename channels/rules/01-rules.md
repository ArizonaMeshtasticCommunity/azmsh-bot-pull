**Welcome to the Arizona Meshtastic Community!**

Because we are a community revolving around Meshtastic, I have `borrowed` their Rules posting from their server. We stand by their Code of Conduct, and their rules apply here as well.

When you first join, you might not see all our channels. Check out the <id:browse> pane or right-click on the server name and select "Show All Channels" to explore everything this community has to offer. Happy meshing!

**Community Rules**

1. **Be Respectful and Inclusive**: Treat everyone with kindness and respect. Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech of any kind will not be tolerated.
2. **Keep It Appropriate**: Avoid sharing any inappropriate, sexually suggestive, or disturbing content.
3. **Foster a Positive Environment**: Engage in constructive discussions; avoid personal attacks, insults, and negativity.
4. **No Politics**: Politics is a divisive topic that too often creates hostility and fractures communities. To keep AZMSH inclusive, friendly, and collaborative, political posts of any kind are not permitted. It's OK to talk about the policies, but let's be careful to not let the conversation devolve into politics.
5. **Respect Privacy**: Do not share personal information about others without their consent.
6. **No Spam or Disruption**: Refrain from spamming, excessive self-promotion, or other disruptive behavior.
